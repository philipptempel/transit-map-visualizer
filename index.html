Transit evolution

Watch transit networks grow

Every tram line and metro tunnel was built at some point, and plenty were torn up again. These animated maps replay that history year by year, so you can see why a network looks the way it does today: which lines are old, which are new, and which ones quietly disappeared.

How to use it

  1. 1. Pick a map

    Choose one from the list below. Filter by region or type of network to narrow it down.

  2. 2. Press play

    The years roll forward and lines appear as they open. Drag the slider to jump to any year.

  3. 3. Switch layouts

    Geographic shows lines where they run. Schematic morphs them into a tidy metro-style diagram.

  4. 4. Explore

    Toggle modes such as tram or S-Bahn. Scroll to zoom, drag to pan. Station names appear up close.
